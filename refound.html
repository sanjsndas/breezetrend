Frequently Asked Return Inquiries

Standard domestic returns within the 30-day window include a complimentary prepaid shipping label provided by Breezetrendoqe. For international orders outside our standard territory, return transit postage is deducted from the final refund balance.

In the rare circumstance that an item arrives with defects, please submit a claim via our request portal within 48 hours of receipt. We prioritize instant replacements or 100% full reimbursement without any deductions.

Garments marked explicitly as "Final Archive Sale" are sold on a definitive basis and cannot be refunded or exchanged, unless verified as defective upon arrival.

If your order was redeemed through a boutique gift certificate, your refund is credited instantly back to your digital store account as store credit for future collections.
