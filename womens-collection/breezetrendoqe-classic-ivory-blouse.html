Garment Profile

Architectural Cut & Material Composition

The Breezetrendoqe Classic Ivory Blouse balances structured formal geometry with the natural ease of genuine pure silk. Cut with subtle waist suppression and a relaxed shoulder line, it maintains crisp lines throughout busy workdays while draping effortlessly across the torso.

Each blouse features hand-rolled French seams to eliminate interior bulk against sensitive skin, accompanied by calibrated cuff plackets and concealed natural mother-of-pearl closures for minimal, clean styling.

Garment Longevity & Care

Dry clean only. Do not tumble dry. If steaming at home, use low heat with a protective fabric cloth to preserve the natural silk luster and fiber integrity.
