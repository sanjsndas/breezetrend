Tailoring Mastery

Caring For Your Formal Garments

Preserving the natural elasticity of fine wool, the crispness of Egyptian cotton, and the sheen of jacquard silk ensures decades of sharp presentation.

Read Style Journal

Rest & Rotation

Allow tailored wool jackets and trousers at least 24 hours of rest between wears on wide contoured hangers.

Steam Over Ironing

Gentle vertical steaming lifts creases from wool fibers and silk without crushing natural fabric textures.

Cotton Collar Care

Always remove collar stays prior to laundering to prevent fabric wear along critical collar points.

Silk Neckwear Storage

Untie silk ties immediately after wearing and store loosely rolled or draped on a specialized necktie rack.
