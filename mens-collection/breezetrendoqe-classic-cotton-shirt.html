Garment Profile

Architectural Cut & Material Composition

The Breezetrendoqe Classic Cotton Shirt is engineered for seamless versatility across executive boardrooms and formal social engagements. Crafted from finely combed Egyptian cotton yarns, the fabric feels crisp yet supple against the skin, maintaining clean contours beneath tailored jackets and lightweight knitwear alike.

Each piece features a reinforced spread collar that effortlessly accommodates both Windsor and four-in-hand tie knots, complemented by double-stitched armholes and genuine mother-of-pearl buttons anchored securely on cross-stitched plackets.

Fabric Care & Garment Preservation

Machine wash cold on gentle cycle with similar shades. Hang dry immediately or tumble dry low. Use warm iron while slightly damp for pristine crispness.
