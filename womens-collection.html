Fabric & Longevity

Garment Care Standards

Preserving the texture, drape, and color richness of natural silks and pure virgin wool requires gentle handling and professional textile practices.

Read Journal

Silk Maintenance

Always steam pure mulberry silk on low settings or use professional eco-friendly dry cleaning to preserve luminous luster.

Wool Rest Cycles

Allow virgin wool tailoring 24 hours of rest on wide contoured hangers between wears to restore natural fiber bounce.

Pleat Integrity

Store pleated skirts hanging vertically away from cramped wardrobes to keep crisp knife and accordion folds intact.

Seasonal Storage

Enclose structured blazers and delicate dresses in breathable organic cotton garment bags with natural cedar blocks.
