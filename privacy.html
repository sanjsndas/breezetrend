Data Governance & Protection Standards

At Breezetrendoqe, we treat personal data with precision, discretion, and strict security protocol. This comprehensive Privacy Policy outlines the lifecycle of personal information collected through our online marketplace and ordering services.

1. Data We Collect

We collect distinct categories of personal information necessary to deliver authentic boutique goods, fulfill tailored shopping experiences, and maintain secure order transactions:

Identity & Contact Details

Full name, billing address, physical shipping address, phone number, and verified email address provided during checkout or inquiries.

Transaction Records

Order history, purchased items, invoices, delivery tracking codes, and encrypted gateway payment reference tokens.

Technical System Data

IP addresses, browser client configurations, operating system parameters, and session time metrics captured for fraud detection.

Customer Correspondence

Direct communications submitted via our contact forms, support emails, delivery inquiries, and return authorizations.

2. Purpose of Processing

We process your information exclusively under lawful bases defined by applicable data privacy legislation:

  • Fulfillment of Orders: Verification of payments, dispatching shipments, generating authentic tax documentation, and executing delivery notifications via our Delivery Information procedures.
  • Customer Care & Returns: Managing item return evaluations, warranty consultations, and dispute resolutions under our established Refund Policy.
  • Fraud Prevention: Safeguarding clients and payment systems from illicit checkout attempts, fraudulent chargebacks, and automated cyber attacks.
  • Statutory Compliance: Archiving verifiable financial data for fiscal auditing, customs clearance, and statutory revenue reporting.

3. Information Sharing

Breezetrendoqe does not sell, rent, or lease customer data to third-party brokers. We disclose essential data strictly to verified operational partners:

4. Storage & Security Measures

We implement rigorous physical, organizational, and electronic safeguards designed to defend your private records against unauthorized access, destruction, or interception:

TLS 1.3High-grade end-to-end cryptographic transport encryption
PCI-DSSZero local credit card storage; tokenized payments

5. Your Legal Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you are empowered with comprehensive consumer rights regarding the governance of your personal data:

You may request a copy of all structured personal data held in our repository concerning your registered customer profile and transaction history.

You have the immediate right to rectify inaccurate, obsolete, or incomplete shipping and contact information recorded across our system.

You may demand complete deletion of your customer profile, provided historical fiscal records are not mandated by mandatory taxation laws.

You may object at any time to non-essential data processing or request suspension of profile indexing for non-contractual interactions.

6. Data Retention Period

Personal contact data is retained as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide services. Order and invoicing documentation are strictly archived for standard statutory accounting limitation periods (up to 7 fiscal years), after which records are permanently erased using automated overwrite protocols.
