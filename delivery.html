Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my active consignment?

As soon as your parcel is packed and leaves our atelier, you will receive an automated dispatch notification with an end-to-end tracking link and scheduled delivery window.

Are import taxes included for international parcels?

Yes, international checkout automatically itemizes applicable customs, import tariffs, and duties so your order arrives without additional charges at your local port of entry.

Can I amend my shipping address after confirmation?

Address modifications can be requested within 90 minutes of order confirmation. Contact our concierge desk immediately to intercept processing prior to courier handover.

What if a package arrives damaged or delayed?

All consignments carry comprehensive transit insurance. If your package experiences significant delay or handling damage, our support staff provides swift replacement or priority resolution.
