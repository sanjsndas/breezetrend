Article 01

Visual Representation & Color Display

We make every deliberate effort to photograph our garments under calibrated natural and studio illumination to reflect real textures and colors. Article 02

Natural Fibers & Artisanal Variations

Our wardrobe collections emphasize unadulterated linens, raw wool, silk blends, and combed organic cotton. Organic textiles inherently feature subtle slub patterns, irregular yarn thickness, and tonal striations. Such organic nuances are not considered manufacturing defects; rather, they serve as proof of authentic textile integrity and traditional weaving practices.

Customers are encouraged to consult our tailored fit metrics or connect with our stylists via our contact desk before ordering.
